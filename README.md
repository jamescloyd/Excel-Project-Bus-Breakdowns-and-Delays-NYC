# Excel Project: Bus Breakdowns and Delays in NYC

In this project, I acted as a data analyst at the New York Division of Transportation with the primary goal of improving the efficiency and reliability of the city's bus transportation system. New York City has been experiencing a significant number of bus breakdowns and delays, which has been causing inconvenience to commuters and straining the city's public transportation resources.

My task is to analyse the provided data to identify patterns and factors that contribute to these breakdowns and delays by answering the following questions:

1. What are the most common reasons for delays and breakdowns?
2. How do delay times vary by bus company and borough?
3. Is there a correlation between specific days of the week and the frequency of breakdowns or delays?

I then used pivot tables and created charts to answer these questions. Please find the charts and my comments to the questions in the Excel file [here](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1L-hoDO0QYpDDU8zuRxgjXCGywVGCrL76?usp=share_link).
